Plan

How ready are you?

Set your exam dates and target grades. The app estimates your likely score in each subject from your accuracy, how much of the syllabus you've covered and your speed, and tells you where to spend your time. Estimates firm up after about 40 answers per subject.

WAEC/GCE grades: A1 75%+, B2 70, B3 65, C4 60, C5 55, C6 50 (credit), D7 45, E8 40, F9 below 40. Grade boundaries vary slightly each year. The UTME is scored out of 400 (100 per subject).

Weak spots

Where the marks are leaking

Every answer is logged by topic. Topics under 50% are weak, 50–74% are building, 75% and above are strong. Topics averaging over 50 seconds a question are flagged slow, because JAMB allows about 40. Drill a topic to get five fresh questions on it.
